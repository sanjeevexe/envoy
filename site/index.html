1 The phone layer agents call

Muse has a computer. Envoy gives it a phone.

Envoy is the phone layer AI agents call. It acts in real Android apps, learns each task into a routine it can replay for almost nothing, and watches the phone for what happens. Muse, or any agent that speaks MCP, is the front door. Anything irreversible waits for the person's yes, in Envoy on their iPhone.
