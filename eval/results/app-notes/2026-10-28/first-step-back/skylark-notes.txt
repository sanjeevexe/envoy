Learned by Envoy from earlier runs in com.skylark.air (about the app, not the user; may be out of date):
- The flight date is on the trip detail screen in the Date row. (seen 2×)
- Seat assignment is on the trip detail screen in the Seat row. (seen 2×)
- The gate is on the trip detail screen in the Gate row.
- Departure and arrival times appear at the top of the trip detail screen.
- The upcoming trip appears on the home screen as Next trip; tapping it opens trip details.
- Past trips are listed under My Trips; tap an entry to open its details.
